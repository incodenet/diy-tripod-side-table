01 · BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Geometry, joinery & materials

Three flat legs on 120° radial lines give a stable three-point base. Each leg tapers from 3.5″ to 1.5″ and is cut with matching 12° bevels at both ends.

Workshop safety
Secure stock before every cut, keep hands outside the blade path, use guards and push devices, and never freehand a taper on a table saw. If angled joinery is new to you, ask an experienced woodworker to check your setup.

Why parallel bevels
Cutting the foot parallel to the top keeps the foot flat on the floor once the leg is splayed 12°.
